# Agri_watch Architecture Wiki

Pointers to foundational architectural concepts from `agentic-swe-kit/wiki` utilized in Agri_watch:

- **[LLMOps & AI Agents](file:///c:/Users/adi93/Desktop/agentic-swe-kit/wiki/llmops-ai-agents):** Dual-Loop (Inner fast audio reactive loop + Outer deliberative agentic loop), Prompt Steering, and Entity Disambiguation.

- **[Release It! & Resilience](file:///c:/Users/adi93/Desktop/agentic-swe-kit/wiki/release-it):** Circuit breakers, fail-safe degradation on closed mandis, and fallback to local Agmarknet database cache.
- **[Clean Architecture](file:///c:/Users/adi93/Desktop/agentic-swe-kit/wiki/clean-architecture):** Domain agricultural pricing and arbitrage rules strictly decoupled from WebRTC transport infrastructure.
